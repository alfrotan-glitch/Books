## Vocabulary Skill

Adverbs

In this chapter, we saw the words traditionally and easily. These are examples of adverbs. Adverbs can be used to describe verbs in a sentence; they tell us how something is done. An adverb usually appears right before or after the verb it describes, but it can also appear at the beginning or the end of a sentence.
