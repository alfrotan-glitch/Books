## Unit ①

## Chapter 1

calculate {?/krelkyg} leit/ v. to do math: Can you calculate how much money we will need for the trip?

develop {?/d1 1 velgp/} v. to happen, occur: Over the Pacific Ocean, the storm developed from a few rain clouds into a strong typhoon.

exist {?/ig’zist/} v. to be present in the world as a real thing: He thought that if he couldn’t see something, it didn’t exist.

originate {?/o>'ridso} 1 neit/ v. to begin, come from: Automobiles originated in the 19th century.

primary {?/pra1 1 meri/} adj. something that is very or most important to someone or something: His difficulty with language was the primary cause of his problems.

replace /ri’pleis/ v. to take the place of someone or something: Her boss retired, and she replaced him.

situation / {?sitfa’weifan/} n. the way things are at a certain time, what’s happening: The leaders are meeting to talk about the situation in their countries.

variety {?/va’raiyati:/} n. different types of things: That store carries a wide variety of goods, from clothes to furniture.

## Chapter 2

communicate /ka’myu:ns keit/ v. to give information to others: People communicate by spoken or written language or by body movements.

demonstrate {?/'demoan} streit/ v. to show people how something works or how to do it: The trainer will demonstrate how to do an exercise.

formal /'foamal/ adj. very or too proper: He’s a difficult person to get to know because he is always so formal.

get the hang of /get 85 han av/ expression v. to understand how to do something: If you practice hard, you will soon get the hang of it.

introductory / {?intra’daktori:/} n. a small amount of general information about a particular subject: My college has introductory classes for people who are interested in learning a new language.

practical /'praktiksl/ adj. useful: A computer would be a practical gift for a student.

similar /'simala/ adj. almost alike: She has a blue dress similar to yours, but hers has a green collar. stand for /stend foa/ expression v. when a letter or symbol is used to represent a word or an idea: RSVP stands for a French phrase that asks people invited to an event to please send a reply.
