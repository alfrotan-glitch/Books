## Reading Skill

Distinguishing between Main and Supporting Ideas

Every paragraph has a main idea, with supporting ideas that explain the main idea. The main idea is usually in the beginning of the paragraph—but not always! By knowing the difference between main and supporting ideas, you can understand the purpose of a passage more clearly.
