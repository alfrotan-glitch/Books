## Vocabulary Skill

Prefixes in-, im-, and un-

You read the word unbelievable in line 1 of the reading passage. The prefixes

in-, im- and un- mean not, so adding them to a word makes it negative. These can come at the beginning of a verb, adjective, or adverb. Recognizing and remembering what these prefixes mean can help you to understand more words.
