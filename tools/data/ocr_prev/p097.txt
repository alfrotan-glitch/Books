## Real Life Skill

Dictionary Usage Choosing the Right Word

In English, there are many words that are similar in meaning but are not exactly: the same. A good English dictionary has examples that show how each word is used. These examples can help you choose the correct word

A Expect, hope, and look forward to are similar in meaning. Read the dictionary entries below, then complete the following sentences. Explain your choices to a partner.

expect v. to believe very strongly that something will happen Example: I expect it to be sunny. tomorrow, since the weather forecast says there will be good weather for the rest of the week.

hope v. to want something to happen, to believe that there is a possibility that it can happen Example: I hope it will be sunny tomorrow as I really want to go to the beach.

look forward to phr. v. to expect that something will happen and to be very excited about it; to anticipate something Example: / look forward to going to the beach tomorrow with my friends.
