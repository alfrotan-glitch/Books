Understanding Units of Measure

Most of the world uses the metric system to measure things like length and weight. Some countries, though, use Non-metric measures, of a combination of both. The metric system uses prefixes to indicate the size of the number. The [nosh Comman; yer are milli (0.001), centi (0.01), and kilo (1,000). For example, one kilometer is equal to 1,000 meters, while one meter is equal to 100 centimeters.
