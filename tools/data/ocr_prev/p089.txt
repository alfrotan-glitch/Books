## Moti Tip Sucess or Effort or bility? When you suceed, is it

because of your effort or your ability? Sucess can be a combination of both, but effort is perhaps more important. When you suceed, remember that it is because of the time you spent working on it. When you fail, remember it is not because you are not good enough, but because you need to spend more time and energy on the task
