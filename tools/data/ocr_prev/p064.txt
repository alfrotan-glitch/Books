## Vocabulary Skill

Nouns Ending in -logy/-ology

Learning the meanings of root words and suffixes can help you increase your vocabulary. In this chapter, you saw the word gerontology. The word is made up of the root word geronto, which means person, and the

suffix -logy, which means the study of.
