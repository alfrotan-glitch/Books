## Known discrepancies and limitations

1. **Chapter 22's Summary of Key Points contradicts Chapter 22's own body text and its Assess Your Knowledge answers.** The summary says granulosa-lutein cells make estrogen and theca-lutein cells make progesterone; the body text (p4 · 53–54), the figure legend, and the end-of-chapter question all say the opposite — granulosa-lutein cells make progesterone and theca-lutein cells make estrogens. This guide follows the body text and flags the conflict in Chapter 22.
2. **The same summary calls the zona pellucida glycoproteins "ZO proteins".** The body text and Figure 22–6 give **ZP1, ZP2, ZP3** (the figure notes four related glycoproteins). This guide uses ZP.
3. **Chapter 21's clinical box on semen quality reads "oligospermia (ejaculate volume >2 mL)".** That is the opposite of the usual definition (reduced volume) and is almost certainly a typographical error in the book. This guide reports the sperm-density figure the book gives (less than 10–20 million/mL) and does not repeat the volume claim.
4. **Chapter 6 has only four figures in the book** (the smallest atlas set of any chapter); the guide cites all four rather than padding the list.
5. **No source scans are reproduced.** The atlas plates are copyrighted; the guide cites figure numbers, parts and pages so the reader can open the real image, and any schematic in the guide is original and labelled as such.
6. **Some numbers and clinical correlations are supplementary context**, not book statements: for example GFR and daily filtrate volume, bladder capacity and the volume that triggers the urge to void, uroplakin plaque dimensions, and sperm-density thresholds. The book's own figures are used wherever the book gives one; the source of each number can be checked in the chapter's page range.
7. **The 100 MCQs are written for this guide**, in the style of the book's Assess Your Knowledge questions. They are not past examination questions, and no claim is made about how often a topic has been examined.

### Defects found and fixed in this review

1. **Chapter 12, Table 12–1.** The plasma-composition table had the "other solutes" line merged incorrectly, and the regulatory-protein share read `<1%` where the book's table gives `>1%`. Both corrected against the printed table.
2. **Chapter 12, platelets.** The guide gave `2–3 µm`; the book gives `2–4 µm`. Corrected, and the count (`150,000–400,000/µL`) and lifespan (`~10 days`) added.
3. **Chapter 15, enterocyte absorption.** "Taken up by transcytosis" replaced with the book's mechanism — hydrolysis by glycocalyx enzymes followed by absorption across the brush border.
4. **Chapter 17, alveolar cells.** The invented "~95% / ~5%" surface shares replaced with the book's wording ("almost the entire surface" for type I, "a small part of each alveolus" for type II), and the unstated 200 µm alveolar diameter removed.
5. **Chapter 17, lung cancer.** "Four main types" reduced to the three the book actually names.
6. **Chapter 18, skin repair.** Rewritten to follow the book's five-stage sequence. Healing by first/second intention and burn grading are retained but explicitly labelled as supplementary clinical terminology, which the chapter does not use.
7. **Chapter 18, clinical box.** Unsupported details (Munro microabscesses, Breslow thickness) removed and replaced with the book's own examples (psoriasis, skin-cancer statistics, melanoma, Merkel cell carcinoma, albinism, vitiligo, blistering diseases, salty sweat in cystic fibrosis).
8. **Chapter 19, kidney dimensions.** The commonly quoted `12 × 6 × 2.5 cm` is not stated in the chapter; removed rather than passed off as book content.
9. **Chapter 19, clinical box.** Kimmelstiel–Wilson nodules, renal-cell carcinoma and "nociceptor" style additions replaced with the book's own clinical boxes (polycystic disease, glomerulonephritis, proteinuria, diabetic glomerulosclerosis, sickle-cell nephropathy, stones, pyelonephritis, cystitis).
10. **Chapter 20, islet architecture.** "β central, α peripheral" corrected: the book states both are scattered in human islets (the peripheral-α pattern is the rodent figure).
11. **Chapter 20, clinical box.** Hashimoto thyroiditis, Hürthle cells, Cushing and Conn syndromes are not in the chapter; replaced with its actual examples (pituitary adenoma, diabetes insipidus, Addison disease, pheochromocytoma, diabetes mellitus, goiter, Graves disease, hypo-/hyperparathyroidism, pineal tumors).
12. **Terminology and typography.** `Synthesises`, `neighbours` and `favoured` normalised to the book's American spelling; DOCX/HTML language tag changed from `en-GB` to `en-US`.

### Limitations that remain

- **Thirty pages carry no text layer** (full-page plates and cover pages; listed above). The guide makes no factual statement that depends on them, and where a figure sits on one of those pages the guide cites the figure number instead of describing it. They were **not** all visually reviewed, and this report does not claim that they were.
- **No atlas plates are reproduced**, for copyright reasons.
- **The 100 MCQs are original**, written in the style of the book's *Assess Your Knowledge* questions; they are not past examination papers and no frequency claims are made.
- **The book's own errors are flagged, not copied** (Chapter 22 lutein/theca reversal, "ZO" for ZP, the semen-volume definition).
