## 1. Material scientific errors found and corrected

### 1.1 Two facts wrongly deleted by the previous pass — restored

Both were removed as "not stated in the chapter". Re-checking the extracted source text showed the claim was false in each case.

| Chapter | Item | Evidence in the source | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | Kidney dimensions `12 × 6 × 2.5 cm` | "Approximately 12 cm long, 6 cm wide, and 2.5 cm thick in adults…" (part 3, Ch 19 opening) | Restored with the book's wording |
| 17 | Alveolar diameter `200 µm` | "Alveoli are saclike evaginations, each about 200 μm in diameter…" (part 3, Ch 17) | Restored |

The earlier audit's other "not in the source" removals were re-checked in the same way; no further false removals were found in the sections examined.

## 2. Editorial and pedagogical improvements

- **Plates to study** added to all 23 chapters: 136 figure numbers with the subject each caption names. No image is reproduced; the reader is sent to the printed plate. The list is deliberately short (four in Ch 6, which has only four figures; six elsewhere).
- **MCQ layout fixed in every format.** In the first render, options A–D ran together on one line; they are now one option per line in PDF, DOCX, HTML and EPUB.
- **Examiner folklore and guarantees removed**: "asked almost every year", "students lose this mark every year", score bands that implied a predicted examination result. Scoring is now framed as self-assessment.
- **Scope and independence** paragraph added to the front matter: no affiliation, no endorsement, no plates reproduced, not a substitute for the textbook or the course.
- A sentence advertising online extras ("AccessMedicine") was deleted: it could not be verified for this edition and was not needed.
- "Six plates per chapter" corrected to "four to six", matching what the book actually provides.
- **README and COVERAGE corrected**, including an explicit note that two facts had been wrongly removed as "unstated" and have now been restored.
- Front-matter chapter table verified against the actual part structure (seven companion parts; Chapter 24 sits in Part VI, the MCQ section in Part VII).

## 5. Unresolved limitations

1. **Thirty source pages have no text layer** (part 1: 1–3, 20, 29, 46, 76, 79, 86, 142, 166, 174, 203; part 2: 60, 69, 72, 90, 158, 217; part 3: 42, 50, 80, 82, 127, 166, 180, 198; part 4: 44, 67, 71). They were **not** visually reviewed, and no statement in the book depends on them.
2. **Plate titles are register-level, not visual.** They reproduce the subject named by the book's caption; nobody has compared the guide's description with the printed image for every plate in this pass.
3. **Automated text extraction is not proofreading.** Figures and page ranges were cross-checked, but OCR-like extraction can drop characters; a qualified reader should still check numbers in context.
4. **No independent medical or didactic review** and no classroom use. Judgements about what is "high-yield" are editorial.
5. **DOCX/EPUB/HTML visual fidelity is unverified.** Only structure and the PDF were checked.
6. **Copyright and rights are unresolved.** No permission has been sought or obtained. The guide reproduces no plates and no extended passages, but its title and citation practice (book title, author, edition, publisher, ISBN) have **not** been reviewed by a rights holder or lawyer. Commercial distribution would require a licence and an ISBN. This is not a legal-clearance statement.
7. **The book's own inconsistencies** are disclosed rather than resolved; where the textbook is internally contradictory, the guide follows the body text and says so.
