# Image rights and scientific figure policy

**Source update: 12 November 2026. Release status: EDITORIAL CANDIDATE.**

## Current illustrations
This publication's generator creates **twelve project-original schematic teaching plates** (Pillow shapes, labels and cross-section comparisons). They are *NOT authentic photomicrographs*, not representative patient slides, and not drawn to scale. Appendix E also provides **external links** to university slide resources; linking does not confer rights to copy those slides.

Do **not** extract pictures, diagrams, captions, page screenshots or scanned figures from the publisher's *Junqueira's Basic Histology*, 17th edition, merely because the reference PDF is in the owner's Google Drive. That access does not grant republication rights.

## Legally safer routes to real histological photos
1. Photograph slides yourselves, with permission from the photographer, laboratory/slide owner and, where necessary, institution; exclude patient identifiers.
2. Find images whose **individual file** is proven public domain or CC0; keep provenance. Copyright varies by jurisdiction.
3. Use individual **CC BY 4.0** photographs after confirming the rightsholder, license and image-specific third-party exceptions. Attribute creator, title, source and license; identify edits. CC BY-SA images may require ShareAlike licensing of adaptations; assess the exact obligations. CC BY-NC must not be included if the book may be sold. CC BY-ND is unsuitable for modified figures.
4. Obtain written rights-holder authorization if none of the above applies; require rights covering worldwide print/e-book distribution, commercial sale, translations, cropping/annotations and perpetual availability when needed.

Useful guidance: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia . Wikimedia Commons files have **individual** copyright/attribution conditions; the site itself supplies no blanket commercial permission. OpenStax, NIH, NCI and university image banks may have license exceptions; verify each asset rather than assuming that free public viewing permits copying. OpenStax currently publishes guidance for non-commercial licensing; don't treat its content as safe for commercial use without a file- and edition-specific license check.

## Record and approval gate
Before any third-party photo is embedded into the PDF, EPUB, DOCX or HTML, record it in `rights/image_ledger.csv`: filename, stain/species, image author, exact source URL, license and license URL or written permission, source SHA256 and evidence, crop/modification, precise attribution, alt text, scientific verification and reviewer/date. Mark the entry `approved` only after this documentation; block publishing with entries in `unverified` status.

Preserve documented stains and scale bars and never invent scale or magnification. A generated schematic cannot be advertised as a microscope photograph. Figure licenses and acknowledgements must be carried into **all four book formats**, not only one.

## Publication decision
The twelve original teaching diagrams and externally linked real-slide exercises form a copyright-conservative illustration strategy. **No third-party photos were added or licensed by this update.** Commercial release still requires scientific review, verified author/owner attribution, legal/provenance review, and final print/e-reader proof.
