# Publication Preparation and Sign-off — Basic Histology: Smart Study Notes

**Scope.** A single independently authored, Junqueira 17th-edition-aligned histology learning companion for Afghanistan specialist-entrance preparation and classroom teaching. The same manuscript generates PDF, editable Word, reflowable EPUB and offline HTML. This is an **editorial-production file**, not part of the reader-facing book.

## Editorial design — production system

- **Identity:** midnight navy (#102A34), medical teal (#0E6B6B), mint highlight (#79D4C8), light paper and muted slate. Use meaningful hierarchy and restrained contrast; never color-code facts without a verbal label.
- **Cover:** original vector cell/network motif and typographic book title; no publisher-owned histology photos or unauthorized logos. Independent-study language avoids claiming official Junqueira or MoPH endorsement.
- **Reading:** 10.5 pt PDF prose, ~15.4 pt leading, large chapter titles, clearly distinguished memory prompts, accessible table headers, 35 original in-chapter diagrams, one-page clickable PDF contents, and questions whose stems and all four options remain together.
- **Digital:** scalable EPUB type, links and navigation; HTML responsive tables and offline image resources; DOCX title page with teal divider and editable styles.
- **Scientific diagrams:** 23 chapter hierarchy maps plus 12 schematic tissue patterns. They show concepts, **not** actual photomicrographs, and must never be used as proof of a slide diagnosis.

## Human approval gates before public commercial release

- [ ] A named qualified **histologist** has checked each chapter, diagnostic claim, table, figure label and all 100 MCQs **against the assigned Junqueira 17th edition**, with change records and signature.
- [ ] Owner verifies the **dated 1405 MoPH specialty entrance syllabus** and confirms (or removes) each 16/18 chapter specialty label.
- [ ] Legal and publishing-rights reviewer signs image-rights ledger; confirms original illustration attribution and licensed wording/ownership; approves the publication imprint and distribution terms.
- [ ] Owner supplies and approves exact **author/editor name, publisher/imprint, copyright notice, publication year, edition number, contact/website, distribution region**, and any ISBN required by the selected distribution channel. No values are invented in the book.
- [ ] Owner selects format and printing vendor. Printer confirms **trim, binding, page count, paper stock/opacity, gutter, cover spine width, CMYK/spot-color expectations, bleed and safe area**. Generate the final printer-specific cover wrap only from these exact dimensions.
- [ ] Physical print proof checked under normal lighting for small table text, line breaks, notes and all 35 figures; defects signed off.
- [ ] EPUB checked on a small phone screen and at least two real reader engines, including text reflow, figure alt text, table scrolling, dark mode and external links. Independent WCAG/EPUB accessibility review is recommended.
- [ ] Distribution PDF tested in a second renderer, embedded font inventory verified, and PDF preflight signed; do not label an A4 reader PDF a printer-certified PDF/X without a printer-specific preflight.
- [ ] A small group of students and instructors pilot the book. Collect item-level comprehension errors, completion rate and delayed recall; do not advertise guaranteed exam pass rates.

**Release classification:** educational study copy after passing technical and visual checks; **not independently certified for commercial publication** until the human approval gates above are fulfilled.
